## How Long Does Permanent Eyeliner Last?

Permanent eyeliner is generally considered a long-lasting cosmetic tattoo treatment rather than something that remains unchanged forever. Lash Fantasy's current cosmetic tattoo information states that permanent eyeliner can last around **2–3 years**, with a colour touch-up recommended around the 12–18 month mark to refresh the pigment.

Results can vary between individuals depending on factors such as skin, lifestyle, aftercare, and how the pigment settles.

## Permanent Eyeliner Aftercare

Following the recommended aftercare instructions is an important part of the healing process. Lash Fantasy advises keeping the treated area dry for the first seven days, avoiding swimming and sun exposure during healing, avoiding picking or scratching the area, and following the aftercare instructions provided by the artist.

The salon also recommends a follow-up appointment approximately 6–8 weeks after the initial treatment.
